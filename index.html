Privacy Policy

Daily Assist Marketing Automation

Effective date: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Daily Assist Marketing Automation is a personal software application operated by its owner for managing and publishing promotional content to the owner's Facebook Page and Instagram Professional account. It is not offered as a public service to other users.

This policy explains what information the application processes, how it is used, and how it can be deleted.

2. Information Processed

Depending on the features used, the application may process:

  • Meta account and authorization information.
  • Facebook Page identifiers and basic Page information.
  • Instagram Professional account identifiers and basic account information.
  • Access tokens and related authorization details.
  • Promotional videos, captions, hashtags and publishing metadata.
  • Publishing status, post identifiers, URLs and application logs.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only to operate the personal application, including:

  • Connecting the owner's authorized Meta accounts.
  • Identifying the owner's Facebook Page and linked Instagram account.
  • Preparing promotional content and publishing packages.
  • Publishing content after the owner's explicit approval.
  • Tracking publishing results and preventing unintended duplicate attempts.
  • Troubleshooting application errors.

4. Storage and Sharing

Application configuration, authorization credentials, publishing state and logs are intended to be stored locally on the operator's computer. The application does not intentionally provide this information to other users.

The application communicates with Meta Platforms services when required for authorization, account information retrieval and content publishing. Videos, captions, hashtags and other content submitted for publishing are transmitted to Meta as part of those operations.

Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. This application does not control Meta's independent processing.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained locally for as long as it is needed to operate the application and maintain publishing records. The operator can remove locally stored application data and credentials.

6. Data Deletion and Revoking Access

The operator can remove access and locally stored information by:

  1. Revoking the application's access through Facebook settings under Business Integrations or the relevant Meta account settings.
  2. Removing stored Meta access tokens and authorization credentials from the application's local configuration.
  3. Deleting local application data, publishing records, logs and generated files that are no longer required.

Revoking access prevents future API operations that require that authorization. It does not automatically delete content already published on Facebook or Instagram. Published content must be deleted separately through the relevant platform.

Information already received or retained by Meta is subject to Meta's own data deletion procedures and policies.

7. Security

The operator is responsible for securing the computer and local files used by this application. Access tokens and application secrets should be kept confidential and must not be shared publicly.

8. Children's Privacy

This personal application is not designed for children and is not intended to collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the application's features, data processing or integrations change. The latest version will be published on this page.
